# Assembly

## 1. Cut the steel
1. Mark a 34 mm length on the 1½″ × ¼″ flat bar and cut it with a hacksaw (or have the store cut it).
2. File off the burrs and break the sharp edges. It should drop into the base's 38.5 × 34.4 mm window.
3. Optional: paint it or wrap it in tape so it doesn't rust.

## 2. Set the heat-set inserts (top part)
1. Heat a soldering iron to about 220 °C for PLA (about 245 °C for PETG).
2. Put an insert on top of each corner boss hole and press it down gently and straight, until it sits flush with the boss face.
3. Let it cool. Don't wiggle it.

## 3. Fit the NFC tag
1. Write and test the tag first with your phone (Foqos or the NFC Tools app).
2. Peel the backing and stick the tag inside the ring on the underside of the top ceiling. For an on-metal tag, the **ferrite side faces down, toward the steel**.

## 4. Close it up
1. Put the steel in the base's window.
2. Lower the top over the base. The ledge aligns it.
3. Turn it over and drive 4 × M2 × 6 countersunk screws into the inserts. Snug them, but don't overtighten.

## 5. Feet
1. Wipe the 4 round pockets with isopropyl alcohol.
2. Apply the primer to the silicone feet. Put a drop of CA glue in each pocket and press the feet in for 30 s.
3. If your feet are self-adhesive, the adhesive plus the pocket is often enough on its own.

## 6. Test
- Weigh it: expect about 85–90 g.
- Scan the top with your iPhone from 0–10 mm away. If it's unreliable, try an on-metal tag or move the tag closer to the centre.

## Repair
Remove the 4 screws, lift the top off, peel off the tag and replace it, then reassemble. The feet stay where they are.
