## Tools
- Hacksaw (metal blade) + flat file, or have the steel cut at the store
- Soldering iron with a conical tip (for the inserts). Makerspaces usually have one.
- Small Phillips/hex driver to match the screws
- Kitchen scale (optional, to check the weight)
