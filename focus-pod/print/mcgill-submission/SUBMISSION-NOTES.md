# McGill Schulich Library 3D printing request

Upload `pod-top.stl` and `pod-base.stl` from this folder. They're already oriented for printing, so they don't need rotating.

## Paste into the request form
```
2 parts (one small project, ~20 g PLA total, ~1 h 45 min est. on an S5):
- pod-top.stl: already flipped, flat face on the bed. Please don't rotate.
- pod-base.stl: flat side on the bed as supplied.

Material: PLA, any colour (prefer: ______)
Layer height: 0.2 mm (Cura "Normal" is fine)
Walls: 4 lines (1.6 mm wall thickness)
Top/bottom layers: 6
Infill: 100% (it is a weighted part; parts are mostly walls anyway)
Supports: NONE (no overhangs need it)
Adhesion: skirt or brim, either is fine
Scale: 100%, units in mm (each part is 50 x 50 mm)
These parts fit together with 0.2 mm clearance, so please don't scale or apply horizontal expansion.
```

## Before you submit
- Register for the service first (read the regulations and register).
- Your file must be an STL and the job must take under 15 hours. This job is about 2 hours.
- Pick up at the Schulich Library Service Desk once you get the "complete" email.
- Optional: ask for the base only first as a fit test (~45 min), then the top.
