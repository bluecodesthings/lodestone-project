# Bill of materials

Prices are rough CAD estimates (Montreal, late 2026). Check them before ordering. Items marked "shared" come from the same packs as the [Focus Pod](../focus-pod/BOM.md).

| Qty | Part | Spec | Est. per pod |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Printed top | `print/pod-top.3mf`, ~43 g PLA | print |
| 1 | Printed base | `print/pod-base.3mf`, ~27 g PLA | print |
| 1 | ESP32-C3 SuperMini | USB-C, onboard antenna ("ESP32-C3 SuperMini", usually sold in 3-packs for $15–20) | ~$6 |
| 1–2 | Steel plate | 38.1 × 50 × 6.35 mm, cut from 1½″ × ¼″ flat bar (shared) | ~$1–2 |
| 4 | M2 heat-set inserts | M2, 3–4 mm long, OD ≈ 3.5 mm (shared) | ~$0.50 |
| 4 | M2 × 6 countersunk screws | stainless (shared) | ~$0.40 |
| 4 | Silicone feet | Ø10 mm, 2.5–4 mm tall (shared) | ~$0.40 |
| 1 | USB-C cable + 5 V USB charger | anything works; the pod draws well under 0.1 A | probably already owned |

**Per night pod:** about **$10** plus printing, if you already have the focus pod packs.

## Tools
- Hacksaw + file (or have the store cut the steel to 50 mm)
- Soldering iron for the inserts
- A computer with Arduino IDE or `arduino-cli` to flash the board (one time)
